# Tai Wén Academy: Contemporary Chinese, Volume 1

Lessons 02–15 use stable IDs `cc1-v1-l02` through `cc1-v1-l15`. Lesson 01 keeps its existing authored content and progress keys.

## Sources and content

- `data/academy/cc1-source-content.json`: ordered Traditional textbook dialogues and vocabulary, source PDF hash, lesson/page references, verified audio IDs. This file is not generated from website-authored examples.
- `data/academy/cc1-dialogues.json`: separately reviewed Vietnamese dialogue translations, Pinyin, and Simplified script.
- `data/academy/cc1-vocabulary.json`: lesson vocabulary enrichments. The original vocabulary entries stay in the source file. `canonicalVocabularyId` links matching TOCFL words to existing progress records.
- `data/academy/cc1-grammar-outline.json` and `cc1-grammar.json`: 68 grammar points tied to textbook pages, with Tai Wén teaching explanations and examples.
- `data/academy/cc1-lessons.json`: learner-facing lessons with 12 practice, 12 assessment, and 4 dialogue-listening questions per lesson. Questions use the verified dialogue and vocabulary; the source is not presented as an official answer key. Regenerate with `node scripts/build_cc1_lessons.mjs` after reviewing source/enrichment changes.
- `data/academy/cc1-reviewed-content.json`: 28 verified dialogue transcript/translation mappings, generated by `node scripts/build_cc1_transcripts.mjs`. No transcript is shown for other tracks without reviewed text.
- `data/academy/cc1-script-map.json`: reviewed Traditional/Simplified spellings are checked against the Academy teaching-copy conversion map generated by `python3 scripts/build_cc1_script_map.py`. This map is only for Academy-authored mixed Vietnamese/Chinese prompts; mock-test variants remain untouched.

## Audio

The import manifest has 222 source MP3s: 141 textbook and 81 workbook. PDF audio references verify 60 textbook and 80 workbook tracks. Those 140 files are published under `public/academy/cc1/audio/` with unchanged audio bytes and stable IDs. The other 82 tracks, including both `00.mp3` files, remain in ignored private staging because their activity mapping has not been confirmed. `data/academy/cc1-mapping-report.json` lists every pending ID.

`python3 scripts/import_cc1_audio.py` rechecks source archives and mappings. `python3 scripts/publish_cc1_audio.py` publishes verified tracks only. User confirmed rights to publish textbook text and MP3 on 2026-11-11; this permission does not turn uncertain track mappings into verified ones.

## Progress and editing

Lesson progress uses `taiwen-activity:v1:academy:<lesson-id>`, and audio-track progress uses `taiwen-academy:v1:cc1:lNN`. Existing account synchronization and guest merge recognize both prefixes. Editors are authorized through the existing `academy_editors` Supabase RLS policy. The static lesson remains available if the remote content table is unavailable. Do not change lesson, question, vocabulary, or audio IDs after learners have saved progress.

## Validation

Run `node --test tests/academy-source.test.mjs tests/academy-audio.test.mjs tests/academy-progress.test.mjs`, then the full JS/Python tests, `pnpm typecheck`, `pnpm lint`, and `pnpm build`. The source and learning-content tests verify the 14 lesson boundaries, 284 source turns, 524 vocabulary entries, 68 grammar points, audio links, question-answer mappings, and distinct assessment IDs.
